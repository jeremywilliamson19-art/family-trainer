# Installing the Family Trainer App

App address: **https://jeremywilliamson19-art.github.io/family-trainer/**

Requirements for every iPhone:

- iOS 16.4 or newer (Settings → General → About → iOS Version)
- Install with Safari. Other browsers can add home-screen icons, but Safari is the one that reliably delivers notifications.

## Part 1: Jeremy (admin). Do this first.

You must be the first person to sign in. The first account becomes the household admin.

### A. Install on your iPhone

1. Open Safari and go to https://jeremywilliamson19-art.github.io/family-trainer/.
2. Tap the Share button (the square with an up arrow, at the bottom of the screen).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**. If you see **Open as Web App**, make sure it's on. Tap **Add**.
4. Close Safari. From now on, always open the app from the home-screen icon, not from Safari.
5. Open the app from the icon and enter your email. Type the 6-digit code from the email into the app. Don't tap anything in the email.
6. Tap **Enable notifications**, then tap **Allow** on the iPhone prompt.
7. Tap **Send test notification** and confirm it arrives. Then tap **Continue**.
8. In **Settings**: set your gym and home workout days, your home equipment, and the foods you don't eat (beans and legumes are pre-filled). Age, height, weight and goals are asked in the nutrition update.

### B. Set up the household

1. Go to the **Family** tab.
2. Invite Amanda: enter her name and email, then tap **Add to household**. No email is sent. Tell her to open the app and sign in with that address.
3. Create your son's account: tap **Add child**. Enter his name, age, height, and weight. Pick his sports and training days, and choose whether in-season mode is on.
4. Leave his page open for Part 3. You'll generate his pairing code with his phone in your hand.

### C. Allow camera access (for barcode scanning, coming in the nutrition update)

1. The first time you tap **Scan barcode**, tap **Allow**.
2. If you tapped **Don't Allow** by accident: Settings → Apps → Safari → Camera → Allow. Then close the app fully (swipe it away) and reopen it.

### D. Set up slow-motion recording (for agility testing)

1. Settings → Camera → Record Slo-mo → choose **1080p at 240 fps**.
2. To film a test: open the Camera app, swipe to SLO-MO, and record. In the trainer app, go to Tests, pick the test, and tap **Upload video** to choose the clip from Photos.

## Part 2: Amanda

### A. On her iPhone

1. Open Safari and go to https://jeremywilliamson19-art.github.io/family-trainer/.
2. Tap Share → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
3. Close Safari and open the app from the home-screen icon.
4. Enter the email Jeremy added. Type the 6-digit code from the email.
5. Tap **Enable notifications** → **Allow**, then send a test notification.
6. In Settings → **What I track**, she can turn off Food and Workouts to use the recipe book only, and turn them back on later.

### B. On a laptop (easiest for entering recipes)

1. Open any browser (Safari, Chrome, Edge) and go to https://jeremywilliamson19-art.github.io/family-trainer/.
2. Sign in with her email and the 6-digit code.
3. Bookmark the page. There's nothing to install. Recipes she saves here show up on everyone's phones right away.
4. To import a recipe from a website: Recipes → Import from link → paste the address → confirm the ingredients.

## Part 3: Your son

Do this together, with his phone in your hand.

### A. Check Screen Time first (if he has restrictions)

If his phone uses Screen Time, make these changes on his phone, or from your phone under Family:

1. Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions → App Store, Media, Web & Games → Web Content. If it's set to "Limit Adult Websites" or "Only Approved Websites", add https://jeremywilliamson19-art.github.io/family-trainer/ to the Always Allow list.
2. Make sure Safari isn't blocked under Allowed Apps.
3. If he has app time limits, add the trainer app to Always Allowed after it's installed, so reminders aren't blocked.

### B. Install

1. On his phone, open Safari and go to https://jeremywilliamson19-art.github.io/family-trainer/.
2. Tap Share → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
3. Close Safari and open the app from the home-screen icon.
4. Tap **I have a pairing code**.
5. On your phone: **Family** → his name → **Generate pairing code**. Type the code into his phone within 15 minutes.
6. Tap **Enable notifications** → **Allow**, then send a test notification.
7. Allow camera access the first time he scans a barcode (same fix as Part 1C if he taps Don't Allow).

His account stays signed in. If he's ever signed out, generate a new pairing code.

## Troubleshooting (all devices)

**Notifications aren't arriving**

1. Make sure the app was opened from the home-screen icon. Notifications don't work from a Safari tab.
2. Settings → Notifications → find the app → turn on Allow Notifications.
3. Check that a Focus mode (Sleep, Work, Do Not Disturb) isn't silencing it. Add the app to the Focus's allowed list if needed.
4. Low Power Mode can delay notifications.
5. Still nothing: in the app, go to Settings → Devices & alerts → **Fix it**. Or delete the home-screen icon, reinstall from Safari, and enable notifications again. Your data is saved on the server, so nothing is lost.

**The app opens in Safari instead of full screen.** You're opening a bookmark, not the installed app. Delete it and repeat the Add to Home Screen step.

**Signed in in Safari but not in the app.** This is normal on iPhone, because Safari and the home-screen app don't share sign-ins. Sign in inside the app with the 6-digit code.

**Seeing an old version after an update.** Swipe the app closed and reopen it. If it's still old, wait a minute and repeat. Updates install automatically on the next open.

**New phone.** Install fresh using the steps above and sign in. All your data comes back. For your son's phone, generate a new pairing code.
